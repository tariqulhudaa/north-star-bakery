Our Breads, Pastries, and Cakes

Everything on this page is baked by hand in our kitchen. Prices are listed as ranges because sizes and flavors vary. We do not offer online checkout. To reserve an item, please send us a pre-order request and pay when you pick up.

Breads

Our breads are slow fermented for deeper flavor and a crisp, crackly crust.

Price range: $6 to $12 per loaf.

  • Country Sourdough: Our everyday loaf with a tangy, chewy center.
  • Whole Wheat Honey: Soft and lightly sweet, made with local honey.
  • Seeded Rye: Hearty rye packed with sunflower, flax, and sesame seeds.
  • Rosemary Focaccia: Olive oil bread topped with fresh rosemary and sea salt.

Pastries

Perfect for a quick breakfast on your commute or a weekend treat with the family.

Price range: $3 to $6 each.

  • Butter Croissants: Flaky, golden layers made with real butter.
  • Cinnamon Rolls: Soft rolls swirled with cinnamon sugar and topped with vanilla glaze.
  • Seasonal Fruit Danishes: Filled with fruit from local farms that changes each season.
  • Cookies: Chocolate chunk, oatmeal raisin, and sugar cookies baked fresh daily.

Cakes

From small birthday cakes to tiered cakes for weddings and community events, we bake every cake to order.

Price range: $25 to $150, depending on size and design.

  • Classic Layer Cakes: Chocolate, vanilla bean, or carrot cake in 6-inch to 10-inch sizes.
  • Celebration Cakes: Custom decorated cakes for birthdays, graduations, and showers.
  • Event and Sheet Cakes: Serves 20 to 100 guests, ideal for event planners.

Cakes require at least 72 hours' notice.
