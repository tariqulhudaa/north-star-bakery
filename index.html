Welcome to North Star Bakery

Fresh From Our Oven to Your Table

For over ten years, North Star Bakery has been the warm corner spot where neighbors start their mornings. We bake everything by hand in small batches, from crusty sourdough to buttery croissants, so there is always something fresh waiting when you walk through our door.

What We're Known For

  • Slow-fermented sourdough made with a starter we have fed every day since we opened.
  • Flaky morning pastries that commuters grab on the way to work.
  • Custom celebration cakes for birthdays, weddings, and community events.
