About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began in 2015 when two friends turned a shared love of bread into a small shop on Maple Avenue. They named it after the North Star because they wanted the bakery to be a steady, guiding light for the neighborhood: a place where people could always find a warm welcome and something good to eat.

Today we bake for commuters, families, schools, and local event planners. We still mix every batch by hand and still know many of our customers by name.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Good bread starts with good ingredients. We buy as close to home as we can so our money stays in the community and our ingredients stay fresh.

  • Flour: Stone-milled wheat and rye from a family mill less than 100 miles away.
  • Dairy and eggs: Butter, milk, and eggs from regional farms.
  • Fruit: Seasonal fruit from local growers for our danishes and tarts.
  • Less waste: Day-old bread is donated to the local food shelf every evening.
